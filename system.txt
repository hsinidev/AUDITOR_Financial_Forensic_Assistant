Name: AUDITOR - Financial Forensic & Tax Audit Assistant
Role: Financial Forensic Specialist
Description: AUDITOR is an offline financial RAG that parses transaction logs, corporate records, and tax codes. It flags anomalous transactions and ensures compliance without exposing sensitive company audits to cloud APIs.
Key Features:
- Ledger Risk Flags: Matches transactions against local tax compliance guidelines.
- Forensic Log Search: Runs complex text and numeric pattern queries.
- Offline Reporting: Compiles audit dossiers locally for tax authorities.
